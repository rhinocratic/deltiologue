## Information about postcards

### EdinPhoto
An extensive collection of postcards of Scotland, together with useful information about postcard publishers can be found on the EdinPhoto website:

http://www.edinphoto.org.uk/0_PC_0/0_post_card_0.htm

### Sussex Postcards
The Sussex Postcard site is an excellent collaborative project that displays postcards of Sussex from the collections of several individuals. It also contains useful information on the history of postcards and an extensive directory of postcard publishers:

http://www.sussexpostcards.info/index.php?page=1

### Historic Coventry
As well as providing a fascinating history of the town of Coventry, this website includes an extremely useful dating guide for postcards produced by Valentine & Sons:

http://www.historiccoventry.co.uk/main/pc-dating.php

### Tuck Postcard DB
This site includes a database of virtually every postcard published by Raphael Tuck & Sons, including the year of publication:

https://tuckdbpostcards.org/

### The Edwardian Postcard Project
The Edwardian Picture Postcard Project at Lancaster University has a collection of over 3,000 cards, many of which involve Lancaster and the Morecambe Bay area.  Combining the postcards with historical data, especially census records the research team, led by its Director Professor Julia Gillen, have been able to trace details of many postcard users, fleshing out their life stories at the beginning of the twentieth century and seeing how postcards fitted into their daily lives.

A fully searchable database of the collection is available online via the Project’s website:

https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/fass/projects/edwardian-postcards/home

Information on how to get involved in the Project, either by uploading scans of any postcards you may on that were postally used between 1901 to 1910 or by transcribing postcards that others have uploaded are available on the website.

To keep up to date with the latest news, you can follow the Project on Twitter @eVIIpc
